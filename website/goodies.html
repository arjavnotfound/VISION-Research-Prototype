Wallpapers

Click on the images below to download the wallpapers.

One day it occurred to me that a cursor shape should be able to tesselate, and so I had to make these.

They're not exactly designed as wallpapers (hence the weird aspect ratios), and they're a bit garish, color-wise, but with some tweaks in an image editor you could shade them to your liking.

I've also made some wallpapers that are specifically V.I.S.I.O.N. themed for my personal use, to feel cool and thematic as I work on the project, but I haven't uploaded them yet.
